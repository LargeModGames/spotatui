- **MilkDrop presets on the visualizer screen**: builds with the `milkdrop` cargo feature press `m` to play MilkDrop presets through the tidewarp engine, with shader presets on the GPU, drawn as real pixels in terminals with Kitty or Sixel graphics. `[` and `]` change the preset, and every `.milk` and `.milk2` file in `~/.config/spotatui/milkdrop` plays next to the six built-in presets. JPEG, PNG and TGA images in that folder go to the shader presets that sample them by file name.
