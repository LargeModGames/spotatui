- **The Connect device comes back after sleep/wake with slow DNS**: after a laptop wake, DNS can still fail while spotatui rebuilds the native Connect device. The rebuild was tried once and then given up on, so spotatui stayed absent from Spotify's device list until a restart, even after the network recovered. Transient build failures now retry with backoff (5 s up to 60 s) while spotatui still wants native playback, and stop when the network is up and the device re-registers, or immediately for a missing login or credentials Spotify rejected. When every Spotify access point answers "try another access point", the login reads as retryable rather than rejected, so the retries continue until one answers.
