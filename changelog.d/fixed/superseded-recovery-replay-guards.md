- **A superseded recovery no longer resumes playback for a player nobody drives**: recovery replayed its saved intent (resume the track, replay a parked request) after the lock that installed the rebuilt player was released, so a handoff to another device, a forced recovery, a Soloist switch or a newer request that took the slot in that gap did not stop the stale replay, and playback continued on a player spotatui no longer drives. A delayed recovery request could also arrive after a newer recovery finished and replay the old session's intent over the new player the same way. Both replay paths now check under their own lock that the request still owns the recovery identity and the player is still the published one, and skip the replay when they do not.
