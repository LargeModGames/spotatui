# Credits

## Libraries

spotatui stands on these projects, among many others (see `Cargo.toml` and `gui/package.json`):

- [ratatui](https://github.com/ratatui/ratatui) and [crossterm](https://github.com/crossterm-rs/crossterm): the terminal UI.
- [librespot](https://github.com/librespot-org/librespot): Spotify playback and Spotify Connect, through our fork [spotatui-librespot](https://github.com/LargeModGames/spotatui-librespot).
- [rspotify](https://github.com/ramsayleung/rspotify): Spotify model and id types.
- [rodio](https://github.com/RustAudio/rodio) and [symphonia](https://github.com/pdeljanov/Symphonia): audio output and decoding for the other sources.
- [tokio](https://github.com/tokio-rs/tokio): the async runtime.
- [clap](https://github.com/clap-rs/clap): the command line.
- [mlua](https://github.com/mlua-rs/mlua): the Lua plugins.
- [Tauri](https://github.com/tauri-apps/tauri): the desktop app.
- [cava](https://github.com/karlstav/cava): the visualizer's core, vendored.
